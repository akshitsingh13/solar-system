Controls

🖱️ Mouse & Touchpad

Using a Mouse

  • Orbit: Left-click & drag
  • Zoom: Scroll mouse wheel
  • Pan: Right-click & drag

Using a Touchpad

  • Orbit: One-finger drag
  • Zoom: Two-finger pinch
  • Pan: Two-finger swipe
Tip: When a planet camera is active, controls will automatically orbit around that body.

👆 Touch Controls

Gestures

  • Orbit: One-finger drag
  • Zoom: Two-finger pinch
  • Pan: Three-finger drag

Navigation

  • Use the buttons above to jump to any planet
  • Free Cam unlocks the camera from the tracked body
  • System resets to the full solar system view
Tip: Tap a planet button first, then use Free Cam to explore freely around that area.
